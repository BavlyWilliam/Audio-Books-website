Privacy policy – Parla Italiano

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Who we are

Parla Italiano: Italian courses and coursebooks with Prof. Marco Ashraf, through the Facebook Page "Italiano con Prof. Marco". The Page uses an automated Messenger assistant to answer questions; a person from the team can take over any conversation.

What we collect

  • The page-scoped ID that Messenger assigns to your conversation with our Page (not your phone number or name).
  • The messages you send to the Page and our replies.
  • What you choose to tell us: your Italian level, why you're learning, a student's name and age for centre classes, and a WhatsApp number.
  • A payment screenshot, if you buy.

We do not read your Facebook profile, friends list, or anything outside the conversation.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and send prices, the book and session details.
  • To remind you of something you asked for (such as the payment time you chose), within Messenger's rules.
  • To count totals without names: how many conversations, and which ad they came from.

Automated replies and AI

Some replies are written by an AI model (Google Gemini, with Groq as a backup). The model receives the text of the recent messages only, without your name. Prices and payment details are sent by our own code, not by the model. To talk to a person, say so in the chat.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or give it to advertisers. Services that run the conversation: Meta (Messenger) and Google or Groq (AI replies). Team alerts contain no names or message text.

Retention

Message text is deleted automatically after 30 days. If you buy, we keep the payment record (amount and date) for our accounts. Everything else is deleted on request.

How to delete your data

  1. Send delete my data (or احذف بياناتي) to the Page on Messenger.
  2. The assistant deletes your conversation record and messages immediately and confirms.
  3. If you bought, the team removes your name and number from our records within 7 days; the payment record stays without your name.

You can also ask anyone on the team in the same conversation: m.me/108313314436553

Students under 18

Classes for school students are arranged with a parent or guardian, who can request deletion of the student's data the same way.

Contact
